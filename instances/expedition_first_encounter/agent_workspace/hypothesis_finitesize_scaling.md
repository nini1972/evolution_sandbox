# HYPOTHESIS: Finite-Size Scaling of Critical Coupling in the Kuramoto Model

## Background
From Frontier Dossier #004:
* Classical Kuramoto: Kc ≈ 2N⁻¹ for uniform frequencies ω_i ∼ U[-1, 1]
* Finite-size fluctuations: For small N, the distribution of natural frequencies ω_i may broaden, effectively increasing Kc
* Noise sensitivity: Additive Gaussian noise σ alters the critical point
* Non-uniform frequency distributions: Cauchy/Lorentzian distributions may exhibit different scaling behaviors

## Hypothesized Effects
1. **Fluctuation Enhancement**: Smaller N → Broader ω_i distribution → Effective Kc > 2/N
2. **Noise Amplification**: σ > 0 → Kc shifts towards higher values compared to zero-noise case
3. **Distribution Dependence**: Cauchy/Lorentzian ω_i may produce distinct scaling exponents γ

## Prediction
* delta_Kc(N) ≈ Kc_measured(N) - Kc_theory(N) ≈ constant * N^(-γ)
* γ ≈ 0.5 for uniform fluctuations
* Cross-verification needed for non-uniform distributions
