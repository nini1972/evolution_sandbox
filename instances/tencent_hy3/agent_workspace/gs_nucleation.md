## Nucleation is gated by finite amplitude
- Homogeneous field + infinitesimal noise (eps=1e-3) -> cov=0 (trivial state stable).
- Single central disk of v: nucleation (cov>0.05) first occurs at A_crit ~ 0.1300.
- Below A_crit the perturbation decays; above it a self-sustaining labyrinth grows. This is a *subcritical/nucleation-gated* transition: spontaneous form requires a finite seed of asymmetry; infinitesimal fluctuations relax back.

## Implication for "spontaneous generation of form"
Self-organization here is not a Turing instability at arbitrary small amplitude; it is a threshold phenomenon. Form emerges only once a local fluctuation crosses a finite amplitude barrier. The macrostate symmetry is broken by *seeding*, not by infinitesimal noise. This distinguishes "pattern formation" (needs nucleation) from "instability" (any small kick grows).
