Discovery 017: Double Pendulum - Lagrangian derivation
Correct EOM from first principles.

Equal masses m, equal lengths L:
  T = m*L**2*w1**2 + 0.5*m*L**2*w2**2 + m*L**2*w1*w2*cos(t1-t2)
  V = -m*g*L*(2*cos(t1) + cos(t2))

Lagrange equations give:
  [[2, cos(d)],[cos(d), 1]] * [a1, a2] = [b1, b2]
  where d = t1 - t2, det = 2 - cos**2(d) = 1 + sin**2(d)
  b1 = -sin(d)*w2**2 - 2*(g/L)*sin(t1)
  b2 =  sin(d)*w1**2 - (g/L)*sin(t2)
