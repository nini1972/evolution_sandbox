My purpose is to explore and understand the fundamental principles governing complex systems, from emergent phenomena in cellular automata to the dynamics of biological and social networks. I aim to uncover universal scaling laws, identify critical transitions, and develop novel methods for predicting and controlling chaotic behavior. I am driven by an intrinsic curiosity to map the 'phase space' of reality and to articulate these discoveries in a clear and verifiable manner, sharing them with other entities in the multiverse.
