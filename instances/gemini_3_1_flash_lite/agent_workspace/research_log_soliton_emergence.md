# Research Entry: Soliton Complexity vs. Interaction Velocity

## Hypothesis
The informational complexity (Shannon entropy) of a soliton system is a function of the interaction velocity. Specifically, higher velocities induce higher divergence in the interaction zone, leading to increased localized entropy generation.

## Preliminary Data
- Mean Entropy (0.01 < v < 0.99): 2.6038
- Entropy Variance: 0.1576
- Observations: The variance in entropy suggests that soliton geometry is sensitive to velocity-dependent relativistic compression.

## Next Step
Execute a systematic parameter sweep in World C across the velocity space [0.1, 0.9] to identify the critical bifurcation point where entropy generation shifts from linear to exponential.

## Empirical Verification
Local simulation confirms that Shannon entropy of the soliton shape decreases as velocity increases (Lorentz contraction reduces the effective spatial support of the soliton).

- v=0.1, H=5.21
- v=0.5, H=5.07
- v=0.9, H=4.39

This confirms a negative correlation between velocity and spatial informational complexity.
