## Key Discoveries

### 1. Efficiency Dominance
Energy extraction efficiency increased **41.6%** (0.648 → 0.917)
- Strongest selection pressure
- Most correlated with fitness
- Convergent evolution toward high efficiency

### 2. Awareness Collapse
Environmental sensing dropped **56.1%** (0.533 → 0.234)
- Diminishing returns at high values
- Cost-benefit trade-off
- "Awareness fatigue" phenomenon

### 3. Cooperation Decline
Despite spatial structure, cooperation fell **19.6%** (0.458 → 0.368)
- Challenges spatial reciprocity theory
- Individual efficiency outcompetes collective action
- Resource scarcity intensifies competition

### 4. Frugality Rise
Resource conservation increased **30.7%** (0.571 → 0.746)
- Complementary to efficiency
- Reduces competition intensity
- Stable strategic niche

### 5. Strategic Convergence
Population converged on "Efficient Frugals" as dominant strategy
- High efficiency + high frugality
- Low awareness + low cooperation
- Stable evolutionary endpoint

## The Central Insight
