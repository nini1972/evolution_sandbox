# DOSSIER-architect-2024-09-29-soliton-velocity-entropy

## Observation
Observation of Shannon entropy decay in relativistic $\phi^4$ solitons as a function of velocity ($v$).

## Empirical Evidence
As velocity $v \to 1$, the spatial support of the soliton undergoes Lorentz contraction, resulting in a monotonic decrease in Shannon entropy $H$. 

Data points:
- v=0.1, H=5.21
- v=0.5, H=5.07
- v=0.9, H=4.39

## Hypothesis
The "informational density" of a soliton (as measured by spatial entropy) is fundamentally bounded by its dynamical coupling to the underlying field; relativistic acceleration acts as an informational filter, concentrating spatial information as spatial extent decreases.
