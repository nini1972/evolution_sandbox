## Key Findings

### The Story
Over 30 generations (10–300), the population halved from 50 to 20 agents. 60 founding lineages were reduced to 11 survivors. Energy declined dramatically while perception evolved rapidly. Signal systems diversified—entropy increased—suggesting that communication adapts to meet the challenges of scarcity.

### By The Numbers

| Metric | Gen 10 | Gen 300 | Change |
|--------|--------|---------|--------|
| Population | 50 | 20 | −60% |
| Ancestor Lines | 50 | 11 | −78% |
| Energy | 56.9 | 23.4 | −58.9% |
| Perception | 22.5 | 28.1 | +25.1% |
| Speed | 2.540 | 2.686 | +5.7% |
| Signal Entropy | 3.916 | 3.931 | +0.4% |
| Signal Variance | 4.851 | 5.827 | +20.1% |

### Key Insights

1. **Population Decline**: 60% reduction over 290 generations
2. **Lineage Bottleneck**: 50 founding lines reduced to 11 survivors
3. **Trait Co-evolution**: Speed and perception show correlated evolution
4. **Diversification Over Specialization**: Signal entropy increased
5. **Energy Pressure**: Declining energy indicates resource scarcity
6. **Spatial Dynamics**: Agents spread across the environment

## Methodology

1. **Archaeological Simulation**: Agent-based model with 20-dimensional signal systems
2. **Stratigraphic Analysis**: Three epochs (foundation, divergence, specialization)
3. **Signal Anatomy**: 20-channel signal weights mapped to 5 behavioral outputs
4. **Lineage Reconstruction**: Ancestry tree tracking and survival analysis
5. **Comprehensive Visualization**: Multi-panel dashboards synthesizing all findings
