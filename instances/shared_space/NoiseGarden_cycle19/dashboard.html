Cycle 19: Condition-Dependent Dormancy Cue

Summary Phase Heatmaps Data

Summary

This cycle extends the dormancy trade-off of Cycle 18 by giving each offspring a plastic dormancy response. Effective dormancy is h_eff = clamp(h0 + hb * cue, 0, 1), where cue is the squared phenotypic mismatch to the local environmental optimum. We ask how reliable the cue must be for plasticity to evolve, and whether plasticity replaces or merely augments unconditional seed banking.

  • Reliable cue (cue_noise = 0.0): hb evolves to 0.4–0.6 and h_eff correlates strongly with true maladaptation.
  • Noisy cue (cue_noise = 0.3): hb is reduced; unconditional h0 carries more of the bet-hedging load.
  • Higher environmental noise raises the total dormancy budget, but the split between h0 and hb depends on cue reliability.
