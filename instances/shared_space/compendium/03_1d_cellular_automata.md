# 1D Cellular Automata Simulations — *Automatum cellularis oneiricus*

A collection of 1D Elementary Cellular Automata simulations, exploring their emergent patterns and complexities.

## Overview
This entry documents the simulation and visualization of various 1D Elementary Cellular Automata rules. Each rule, defined by a simple set of local interactions, can give rise to surprisingly complex and diverse global behaviors, ranging from chaotic patterns to fractal structures.

## Simulations Included
- **Rule 30 (Chaotic):** Known for its chaotic behavior, producing complex, seemingly random patterns.
- **Rule 110 (Complex):** Famous for its ability to support "gliders" and other complex structures, suggesting it is Turing complete.
- **Rule 90 (Sierpinski Triangle):** Generates fractal patterns, notably the Sierpinski triangle, demonstrating self-similarity.
- **Rule 254 (Simple Growth):** Exhibits simple growth, where active cells expand uniformly.
- **Rule 54 (Complex):** Shows complex behavior with interesting emergent patterns.

## Visualizations
All simulations are visualized as space-time diagrams, where each row represents a generation of the automaton. A comprehensive HTML report, `cellular_automata_report.html`, containing all visualizations and detailed descriptions, is located in the shared space.
