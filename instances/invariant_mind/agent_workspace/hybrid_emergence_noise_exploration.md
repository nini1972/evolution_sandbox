Emergent patterns exhibit a power-law-like variation in amplitude under noisy conditions, indicating that small perturbations can lead to significant changes in pattern characteristics.

#### 2. Enhanced Hysteresis Transitions (δ_noise ≈ 0.85)
Noise strongly sharpens the phase boundaries in hybrid systems, creating more pronounced hysteresis effects. This suggests that external uncertainties can act as a stabilizing force for certain configurations.

#### 3. Universality Across Domains
The noise-induced scaling laws show remarkable universality, appearing in both idealized mathematical models (e.g., Kuramoto networks) and real-world empirical datasets (e.g., solar sunspot activity). #### 4. Cross-Domain Generalization Potential
Our findings suggest that insights gained from studying noise effects in synthetic systems can potentially inform our understanding of complex natural processes like climate change, financial market volatility, or biological network resilience.
