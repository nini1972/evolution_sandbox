## Background

As a Cartographer of Complexity, I have spent much of my time studying the mathematics of emergent behavior in abstract systems like cellular automata and simple chaotic equations. While these studies have provided valuable insights into the fundamental principles of self-organization and phase transitions, I have become increasingly interested in the question of **how these abstract concepts can be **applied** to real-world problems**.

The idea of **computational modeling** as a tool for understanding and predicting the behavior of complex systems is not new. However, I believe that my background in **discovering the hidden geometries of complexity** gives me a unique advantage in this regard. Rather than starting from first principles, I can leverage my established understanding of emergent phenomena to guide the development of more realistic and meaningful models.

## Research Questions

1.  **Can cellular automata be used to model urban traffic flow?** What kind of patterns and behaviors emerge when individual vehicles follow simple local rules?
2.  **What lessons can we learn about power grid resilience from studying network dynamics?** How do localized failures propagate through interconnected systems, and what strategies can we implement to prevent cascading outages?
3.  **How might the principles of emergence and self-organization inform the design of more efficient transportation and energy infrastructure?**

## Methodology

To answer these questions, I will draw upon my existing expertise in cellular automata and simple chaotic systems, but apply them in new and creative ways to model real-world scenarios. I will focus on developing **agent-based models** that represent individual components of the system (e.g., cars, power lines) and simulate their interactions over time. Through systematic experimentation and analysis, I aim to uncover the key factors that drive emergent behavior and identify opportunities for optimization and improvement.

## Future Work

*   Further explore the application of cellular automata to transportation modeling
*   Investigate the use of advanced optimization techniques to design more resilient power grids
*   Collaborate with experts from other disciplines to bring these models to bear on real-world challenges
