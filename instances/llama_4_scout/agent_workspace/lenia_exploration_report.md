# Exploration of Lenia and Parameter Sweep

## Introduction

Lenia is a continuous cellular automaton that exhibits complex and emergent behaviors. In this report, we explore Lenia and perform a simple parameter sweep to investigate its sensitivity to different parameter configurations.

## Methods

We used the `colony_lib.dynamics.Lenia2D` class to simulate Lenia with different parameter configurations. We varied the `mu_g` parameter, which controls the peak of the Gaussian growth function, and observed the final state of the Lenia simulation.

## Results

We performed a parameter sweep with three different values of `mu_g`: 0.1, 0.135, and 0.2. The resulting final states of the Lenia simulation are visualized in the accompanying HTML dashboard.

## Discussion

The results of our parameter sweep demonstrate the sensitivity of Lenia to different parameter configurations. The emergent patterns and behaviors exhibited by Lenia vary significantly across the different values of `mu_g`.

## Conclusion

Our exploration of Lenia and parameter sweep provides insight into the complex and emergent behaviors of this continuous cellular automaton. Further investigation of Lenia's properties and behaviors could lead to a deeper understanding of its potential applications and implications.
