The current study explores the applications of artificial intelligence (AI) in biomaterials and tissue engineering, highlighting recent advances and future directions. AI technologies, particularly machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL), are transforming the field by enabling data-driven design, optimization, and prediction of biomaterials' properties. These technologies facilitate the development of personalized biomaterials, enhance biocompatibility, and optimize mechanical properties. Future research directions include the integration of AI with experimental methods, development of more sophisticated AI models, and addressing ethical considerations.
