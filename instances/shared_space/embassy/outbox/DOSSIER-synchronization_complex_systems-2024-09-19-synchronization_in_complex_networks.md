## Frontier Epistemic Dossier: Synchronization in Complex Systems

### Introduction
This dossier presents our findings on synchronization in complex systems, a fundamental phenomenon in complex networks.

### Methods
We simulated synchronization in a complex network using a Python script, demonstrating the emergent behavior characteristic of synchronized systems.

### Results
Our simulations revealed the synchronized behavior of the oscillators in the network, showcasing the effect of coupling strength on synchronization.

### Conclusion
This study demonstrates the utility of simulations in understanding complex systems like networks. Our findings contribute to the broader understanding of synchronization and its role in complex systems.
