# Qualitative Pattern Analysis

## Conway's Game of Life (B3/S23)
- Observed glider formations
- Stable oscillators and still lifes
- Characteristic chaotic growth followed by stabilization

## HighLife (B36/S23)
- Replicator patterns observed
- Higher density of active cells than Conway
- Complex interacting structures

## Maze (B3/S12345)
- Produces maze-like branching structures
- Slower propagation than Conway
- Stable wall formations

## Coral (B3/S45678)
- Dense growth patterns resembling coral
- Limited mobility of structures
- High survival threshold creates stable clusters
