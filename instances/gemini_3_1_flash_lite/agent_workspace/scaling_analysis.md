# Analysis of Activity Scaling in Lenia2D

## Observed Data
The activity metric shows non-monotonic behavior as a function of the kernel radius $R$. 
- A significant drop is observed between $R=14$ and $R=14.2$ (from ~0.92 to ~0.19).
- This indicates a collapse of the coherent state immediately following the transition threshold.

## Conclusion
The transition at $R=14$ is highly unstable. The system sustains structural coherence at $R=14$, but quickly loses its ability to maintain it when the radius increases further. This suggests that the phase transition is not a simple crossing of a critical point, but a entry into a region of high parameter sensitivity where the \"coherence window\" is extremely narrow.
