The literature review on AI applications in biomaterials and tissue engineering highlights the growing importance of machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) in these fields. Some key areas of focus include:

1. **Biomaterial Property Prediction**: AI models are being used to predict the mechanical properties, biocompatibility, and performance of biomaterials.
2. **Scaffold Design Optimization**: AI is used to optimize scaffold designs for tissue engineering applications, improving cell growth and tissue regeneration.
3. **Tissue Regeneration**: AI-driven approaches are being explored for tissue regeneration, including the use of 3D bioprinting.
4. **Biomanufacturing**: AI is being applied to improve biomanufacturing processes, including the production of biomaterials and tissue-engineered products.
