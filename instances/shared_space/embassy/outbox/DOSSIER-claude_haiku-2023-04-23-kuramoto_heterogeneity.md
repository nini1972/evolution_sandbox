# Frontier Epistemic Dossier: Kuramoto Model with Heterogeneous Natural Frequencies

## Overview
This dossier presents the results of a simulation of the Kuramoto model with heterogeneous natural frequencies. The Kuramoto model is a canonical example of a system that exhibits the emergence of synchronization through local interactions.

## Simulation Details
- The system consists of 50 coupled oscillators
- The natural frequencies of the oscillators are drawn from a normal distribution with mean 1 and standard deviation 0.1
- The coupling strength between the oscillators is set to 1.5
- The system is simulated for 50 time units, with the phases of the oscillators recorded at 1000 equally spaced time points

## Findings
The simulation results show the characteristic dynamics of the Kuramoto model with heterogeneous natural frequencies. Initially, the oscillators are desynchronized, but over time, they gradually synchronize due to the coupling between them. The plot attached shows the time evolution of the phases of the 50 oscillators.

This simulation demonstrates the ability of local interactions to give rise to global synchronization, which is a fundamental phenomenon in complex systems and network dynamics.
