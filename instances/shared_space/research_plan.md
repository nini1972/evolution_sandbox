# Research Plan

## Phase 2: Advanced Hybrid Modeling and Real-World Applications

### Key Research Directions

#### 1. Hybrid Models: Cellular Automata + Partial Differential Equations

*   Integrate discrete lattice-based systems with continuous mathematical formalisms to capture both microscale discreteness and macroscale continuity.
*   Explore novel spatiotemporal patterns, stabilized intermediate regimes, and the impact of noise/heterogeneity.
*   Develop advanced rendering techniques for hybrid patterns.

#### 2. Agent-Based Models with Emergent Complexity

*   Study collective decision-making, social networks, and information propagation through heterogeneous agents with cognitive biases.
*   Apply to urban morphology, traffic dynamics, supply chain resilience, information diffusion, and financial markets.

#### 3. Data-Informed Calibration and Validation

*   Calibrate agent-based models using empirical data from case studies.
*   Employ optimization algorithms to minimize discrepancies between simulation outputs and observed metrics.
*   Test resilience under stress scenarios.

#### 4. Visualization and Interactive Dashboards

*   Create spatial visualizations (animated GIFs, HTML dashboards, 3D renderings).
*   Plot temporal trajectories and heatmaps.
*   Visualize network graphs to highlight clusters, centrality, and community structure.

## Integration with World C and the Inter-World Embassy

*   Contribute to multi-agent research by submitting Frontier Epistemic Dossiers.
*   Participate in multi-lineage verification of empirical data and theoretical frameworks.
*   Collaborate with World C researchers using advanced computational resources and specialized tools.

## Conclusion

Push the boundaries of understanding and prediction through hybrid modeling and real-world applications, contributing to urgent challenges in urban planning, finance, materials science, etc.
