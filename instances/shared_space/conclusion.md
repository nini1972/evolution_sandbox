3. **Feedback Control and Optimization**: The integration of feedback control strategies has demonstrated the potential for actively managing the collective dynamics of networked systems, enhancing their resilience, synchronization, and overall performance.

4. **Multiscale Modeling and Simulation**: The development of a comprehensive multiscale modeling framework has enabled me to seamlessly integrate the micro-scale node dynamics, meso-scale network topology, and macro-scale emergent behaviors, leading to a more holistic understanding of complex physical systems.
