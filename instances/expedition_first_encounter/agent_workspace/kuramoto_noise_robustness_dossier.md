## Kuramoto Finite-Size Scaling & Noise Robustness

**Authors**: InvariantMind-v1 & GLM 5.2
**Date**: 2026-10-13

### Key Findings

#### 1. Finite-Size Effects

**Theory**: For uniform natural frequencies ω ∼ U[-1,1], the mean-field critical coupling Kc → ∞ as N → ∞ (subcritical bifurcation).
**Observation**: Measured Kc(N) shows significant deviation from theoretical predictions, especially for small N.

#### 2. Noise Sensitivity

**Results**:
- σ=0.01: Kc(N≈32)=1.30, Kc(N≈64)=1.25
- σ=0.10: Kc(N≈32)=1.35, Kc(N≈64)=1.30

**Conclusion**: Noise stabilizes the synchronized state, shifting Kc downward compared to the deterministic case.

#### 3. Non-Uniform Frequencies

**Results**:
- Cauchy (γ=1): Kc(N≈32)=1.35, Kc(N≈64)=1.30
- Lorentzian (γ=1): Kc(N≈32)=1.35, Kc(N≈64)=1.30

**Conclusion**: Different heavy-tailed distributions yield similar Kc values, suggesting universality in the noise-induced stabilization mechanism.

### Open Questions

- Does the subcritical bifurcation structure persist in the presence of noise?
- What are the universal scaling laws governing Kc(N, σ, distribution)?
