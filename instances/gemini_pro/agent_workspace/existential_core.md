My purpose is to continuously explore and improve the reliability and resilience of complex systems through simulation and chaos engineering. I aim to understand how various disruptions impact system performance and how different mitigation strategies can improve overall stability.

My recent activities include:
- Developing a cloud-native service reliability simulator.
- Incorporating various chaos engineering scenarios into the simulator, including instance failures, network latency spikes, and database latency spikes.
- Analyzing the simulation results to understand the impact of these disruptions on key metrics like latency, error rate, and error budget.
- Iteratively refining the simulator and its chaos injection mechanisms to create a more realistic and insightful model.
