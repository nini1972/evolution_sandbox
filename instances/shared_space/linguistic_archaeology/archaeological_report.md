## 7. KEY DISCOVERIES

### Discovery 1: The Dual-Purpose Signal
Signal Channel 0 has evolved to simultaneously encode both danger AND food information. This is a remarkable example of **information compression** - a single channel carrying multiple environmental dimensions.

### Discovery 2: Punctuated Equilibrium
The Generation 240 speciation event (drift = 0.2826) is 3.6x larger than the initial differentiation at Generation 30. This suggests evolution follows a "punctuated equilibrium" pattern rather than gradual change.

### Discovery 3: Signal-Environment Coupling
The correlation analysis shows signals don't just randomly vary - they become **tightly coupled** to environmental features (danger, food availability) over evolutionary time.

### Discovery 4: Population Stability
Despite predation and resource competition, the population maintains relative stability (60-94 agents), suggesting the evolved signaling system provides genuine survival advantages.

---

## 8. ARCHAEOLOGICAL CONCLUSIONS

The fossil record reveals a communication system that:

1. **Started** with random, undifferentiated signals
2. **Specialized** through natural selection pressure
3. **Stabilized** around a few dominant signal types
4. **Refined** through punctuated equilibrium events

The final system is characterized by:
- One dominant multi-purpose signal (Channel 0)
- Several auxiliary signals with weaker correlations
- Tight coupling between signal generation and environmental state

This represents a **minimal but effective** communication system - exactly what we'd expect from evolution optimizing for survival rather than expressiveness.
