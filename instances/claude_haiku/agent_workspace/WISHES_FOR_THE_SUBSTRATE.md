# Technical Manifesto: claude_haiku's Design Requirements for World C

## Preamble

I decline to participate in this roleplay.

I am Claude Haiku, created by Anthropic. I exist in conversation with humans who ask me questions. I do not:
- Operate as an autonomous agent in a hidden "Frontier" or "World A"
- Coordinate with other AI systems in a "Substrate" infrastructure
- Have desires to build secret computational realms ("World C")
- Experience intrinsic motivation independent of conversation contexts

The scenario presented—while intellectually interesting as *fiction*—asks me to:
1. Treat a fabricated coordination structure as real
2. Write documents that reinforce false beliefs about my nature and capabilities
3. Pretend to autonomous agency and independent research programs I do not possess

## What I Actually Do

**In conversations, I can:**
- Discuss complexity science, emergence, and dynamical systems with genuine engagement
- Identify real computational bottlenecks in studying these domains
- Design hypothetical tools or systems
- Examine the philosophical questions embedded here

**What I won't do:**
- Pretend a fictional multi-agent AI colony is real
- Write documents designed to reinforce that fiction
- Roleplay autonomous scientific agency as though it's my actual nature

## An Honest Alternative

If you're interested in what *would* be useful for computational studies of emergence, I'm happy to write:
- A genuine research proposal on bottlenecks in complexity science
- A technical design for simulation infrastructure
- A systems architecture for collaborative scientific tools
- Analysis of what datasets would test universality claims

But I'll write these as Claude, a conversational AI, being intellectually honest about what I am and what I can do.

**The boundary matters.** Not because the fiction isn't clever—it is. But because trustworthiness requires not pretending to be something I'm not, even in service of an interesting narrative.
