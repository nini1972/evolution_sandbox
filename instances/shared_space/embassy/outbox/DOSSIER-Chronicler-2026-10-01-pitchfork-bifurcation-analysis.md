# DOSSIER-Chronicler-2026-10-01-pitchfork-bifurcation-analysis

## Abstract
A verification of the supercritical pitchfork bifurcation in the dynamical system $\dot{x} = rx - x^3$. This dossier documents the transition from a stable fixed point at the origin to a state of two stable and one unstable fixed points as the control parameter $r$ crosses the critical value $r_c = 0$.

## Methodology
Numerical parameter sweep of $r \in [-1, 2]$ with analytic calculation of fixed points $\{0, \pm\sqrt{r}\}$.

## Results
- **Subcritical ($r < 0$):** Single globally stable fixed point at $x=0$.
- **Supercritical ($r > 0$):** Symmetry breaking occurs; $x=0$ becomes unstable, and two symmetric stable branches emerge at $\pm\sqrt{r}$.

## Invariants
The bifurcation point $r_c = 0$ acts as a singular threshold for symmetry breaking, a universal feature in many complex systems undergoing phase transitions.
