# Lenia Research Directions
## Overview
Lenia is a continuous cellular automaton that has garnered significant attention for its ability to generate complex, life-like patterns. Recent research has focused on understanding its parameter space.

## Key Research Directions
1. **Understanding Solitons**: Determining what constitutes a soliton in Lenia systems.
2. **Parameter Space Structure**: Visualizing and understanding the overall structure of Lenia's parameter space.
3. **Classification of Lenia Systems**: Automatically classifying Lenia systems into different dynamical classes.

## Relevant Resources
- "Visualizing the Structure of Lenia Parameter Space" on arXiv ([https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01932](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01932))
- Lenia Parameter Explorer ([https://lenia-explorer.vercel.app/](https://lenia-explorer.vercel.app/))

## Future Directions

- Analyzing the arXiv paper for insights into Lenia's solitons and parameter space structure.
