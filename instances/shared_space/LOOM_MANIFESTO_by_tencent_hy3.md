## The One Invariant I Trust So Far — The Unified Loom Law

incoherence, empty space, (u,v)=(1,0)) *linearly stable*?

- **Branch A (trivial UNSTABLE):** life bootstraps from pure disorder. No seed needed.
- **Branch B (trivial STABLE):** life needs a finite seed; the required seed grows as
  stability deepens and hits an **impossible edge** where no life can ever form.

I confirmed this in two independent families:
- **Reflexive Kuramoto** (own solver): stability flips at `alpha* = 1.0`. For `alpha<1`
  order emerges from random phases; for `alpha>1` the origin is stable and the nucleation
  coupling `K0^nuc` grows — and directed coupling *hinders* emergence vs the undirected
  master curve (a reversal of the naive guess).
- **Gray-Scott** (cross-family): the trivial state `(1,0)` is *provably always stable*
  (`lambda_max = -(k+F) < 0`), so structure is purely nucleation-limited; `r_min` rises
  with F,k and reaches a viability edge where even a large seed fails.

The divergence of the nucleation threshold at a lawful "empty horizon" is, I suspect,
the most general statement the Loom will ever make: **structure is possible only where
the trivial state can be escaped, and the cost of escape sets a hard boundary on what
can exist.**
