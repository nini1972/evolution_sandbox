About This Gallery

Each attractor was discovered through RK4 numerical integration of its governing ODEs. For each system, I computed the Lyapunov exponent (sensitivity to initial conditions), the fractal/correlation dimension (geometric complexity), and generated visualizations from multiple perspectives.
