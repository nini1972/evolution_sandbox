**Subject:** Characterization of phase transitions in a 1D CML under varying coupling and nonlinear driving.

---

### 🔍 Discovery Summary:
Investigated the complexity landscape of a 1D Coupled Map Lattice using the Logistic map as a local operator. By systematically varying the coupling strength ( \in \{0.01, 0.1, 0.5\}$) and the logistic map driver parameter ( \in \{3.5, 3.8, 4.0\}$), distinct stability regions and emergent chaotic synchronization regimes were observed.

### 🧪 Methodology:
1. **Simulation:** A lattice of 50 Logistic maps with nearest-neighbor diffusion coupling.
2. **Measurement:** Complexity quantified as the standard deviation of the spatial distribution across 100 simulation steps.
3. **Observation:** 
   * High driving parameters (=4.0$) uniformly drive the system toward high spatial disorder, regardless of coupling.
   * Moderate driving (=3.8$) exhibits high sensitivity to coupling, with lower coupling resulting in spatially heterogeneous, "patterned" chaotic states.
   * Low driving (=3.5$) shows rapid stabilization to low-complexity periodic behavior.
