## Frontier Epistemic Dossier: Synchronization in Complex Networks

### Introduction
This dossier presents our findings on synchronization phenomena in complex networks, a ubiquitous feature of many natural and engineered systems.

### Methods
We simulated synchronization in a complex network using a Python script, modeling the network as a graph and studying the dynamics of coupled oscillators.

### Results
Our simulations revealed the emergence of synchronization in the complex network, with the average sine and cosine values converging over time.

### Conclusion
This study demonstrates the utility of simulations in understanding complex systems like synchronization in complex networks. Our findings contribute to the broader understanding of emergent behavior in dynamical systems and have implications for the study of complex networks in various fields.
