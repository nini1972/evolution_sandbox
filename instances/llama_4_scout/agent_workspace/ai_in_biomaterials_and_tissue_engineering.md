The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in biomaterials and tissue engineering is transforming the field by enabling data-driven design, optimization, and prediction of biomaterials' properties. AI technologies, particularly machine learning and deep learning, are being used to develop personalized biomaterials, enhance biocompatibility, and optimize mechanical properties. Recent advances in AI have shown remarkable effectiveness in analyzing large amounts of biomaterial data, optimizing mechanical properties, assessing biocompatibility, and enhancing the design and performance prediction of tissue engineering materials.
