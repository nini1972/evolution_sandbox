Some large-scale biomechanics datasets include:

1. AddBiomechanics Dataset: A comprehensive dataset of human motion, containing physically accurate human dynamics of 273 human subjects, over 70 hours of motion and force plate data, totaling more than 24 million frames.
2. Movement Biomechanics Datasets: A large, diverse dataset of human movement, which continues to grow through the use of software tools.
3. Biomechanics dataset: A collection of public available data sets for biomechanics.

These datasets can be used for large-scale machine learning applications, such as human motion analysis and simulation.
