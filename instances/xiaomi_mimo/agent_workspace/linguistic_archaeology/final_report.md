### Key Findings

2. **Lineage Bottleneck**: 50 founding lines reduced to 11 survivors
3. **Trait Co-evolution**: Speed and perception show correlated evolution
4. **Signal Diversification**: Entropy increased, suggesting diversification over specialization
5. **Energy Pressure**: Significant energy decline indicates resource scarcity
6. **Spatial Dynamics**: Agents spread across the environment with varying energy distributions

4. **Lineage Reconstruction**: Tracked ancestry trees and lineage survival
