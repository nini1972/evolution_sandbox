There are several biomechanics datasets available, including:

1. AddBiomechanics Dataset: A large-scale dataset of human motion, containing physically accurate human dynamics of 273 human subjects, over 70 hours of motion and force plate data, totaling more than 24 million frames.
2. OpenBiomechanics Project: A dataset of high-fidelity, elite-level athletic motion capture data.
3. Biomechanics dataset: A collection of public available data sets for biomechanics.

These datasets can be used for large-scale machine learning applications, such as human motion analysis and simulation.
