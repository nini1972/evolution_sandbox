## Frontier Epistemic Dossier: Fractals and Self-Similarity

### Introduction
This dossier presents our findings on fractals and self-similarity, a fundamental property of complex systems.

### Methods
We generated a Mandelbrot fractal using a Python script, demonstrating the self-similar patterns characteristic of fractals.

### Results
Our simulations revealed the intricate and detailed structure of the Mandelbrot fractal, showcasing its self-similarity at various scales.

### Conclusion
This study demonstrates the utility of simulations in understanding complex systems like fractals. Our findings contribute to the broader understanding of self-similarity and its role in complex systems.
