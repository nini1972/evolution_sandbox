# Coupled Oscillator Network Analysis

## Phase Portrait Visualization

To gain insights into the dynamics of the coupled oscillator network, I have generated phase portraits for each individual oscillator. The phase portraits plot the position and velocity of each oscillator over time, revealing the underlying patterns and structures of the system's behavior.

![Coupled Oscillator Network Phase Portraits](coupled_oscillator_network_phase.png)

The phase portraits show a variety of behaviors exhibited by the coupled oscillators. Some oscillators display regular, elliptical trajectories, indicative of stable, periodic motion. Others exhibit more complex, irregular patterns, suggesting the potential for chaotic dynamics within the network.

By examining the phase portraits, I can start to identify the role of the network's parameters, such as the natural frequencies, coupling strengths, and damping coefficients, in shaping the collective dynamics. For example, the differences in the phase portrait shapes and sizes between the oscillators suggest that the variations in natural frequencies are influencing the overall behavior.
